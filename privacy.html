Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

The short version: Screenshot Clean processes everything on your device. We collect no personal data, send nothing to any server, and have no account system. Your photos never leave your iPhone.

1. Information We Collect

Screenshot Clean does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or any form of registration.

2. Photo Library Access

Screenshot Clean requests access to your photo library solely to scan for screenshots. This processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's PhotoKit framework.

Your photos are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any server, third party, or cloud service. Access to your photo library is used exclusively within the app on your device.

3. Data Storage

The app stores only minimal preferences locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults (e.g., whether you have completed onboarding). This data never leaves your device.

4. Analytics & Tracking

Screenshot Clean contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no third-party data collection of any kind. We do not track how you use the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Screenshot Clean does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no user-generated content or communication features.

6. Third-Party Services

Screenshot Clean does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
